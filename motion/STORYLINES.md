# Documentary motion graphics: three directions

One direction is used for all five films so they feel like a series.

**Same in all three:** 1920×1080 at 30 fps. Each film is about 40 s and uses the existing ElevenLabs voice (doc1–doc5.mp3, about 24 s), so the voice ends early and the last ~15 s is a visual payoff and an end title over music. Captions come from the existing synced VTT files. CloudSwyft light palette (white/ice ground, navy type, sky #1AA6EC accent, coral #D97757 only on the word "Claude"), Poppins Bold + Open Sans. On-screen words never repeat the voice word for word. Free sound only: the voice plus soft UI clicks and a quiet music bed. The presentation's own music ducks while a video plays.

---

## A. One Desk, One Day (recommended: people recognise a desk and a workday, so the story is easy to follow)

**Device:** a moment in someone's day. **Form:** one continuous take across a single illustrated desk; the camera never cuts, it glides from object to object.
A clean, flat-illustrated desk (monitor, phone, notebook, wall clock, coffee) is home in every film. A sky-blue "work card" is the thing the eye follows: it gets made, travels and lands as finished work.

- **First 2 s (doc 1):** the phone and monitor on the desk light up with notification badges that pile up and spill over the desk edge.
- **Doc 1 · Future of Work:** notifications pile up → the camera pushes into the monitor: search results list → it becomes a chat bubble → becomes an assistant panel → the pile folds into three finished files (report, sheet, deck) on the desk → title "The AI Revolution."
- **Doc 2 · A Day With Claude:** the wall clock sweeps 9:00 → 11:00 → 14:00 → 16:00; at each hour the camera glides to the monitor, which shows the finished result (cited brief, short summary, a red test turning green, a chart with a person's check mark).
- **Doc 3 · Productivity:** the desk splits in two. The left side churns (tabs multiplying, copy-paste arrows), the right side stays calm (one prompt card). Both cards slide to the same reviewer's hand and check mark → "You stay in the loop."
- **Doc 4 · Idea to Project:** a lightbulb on a sticky note → drops into a folder (Project) with brief and brand files → the folder opens into a live prototype window (Artifact) that changes as you ask → code files stack up and a "Ship" tag lands (Claude Code).
- **Doc 5 · Collaborative:** two hands, yours and a Claude-coral cursor, pass one draft back and forth: Claude drafts, you mark it up, you stamp "Approved" → the camera pulls back to the window, where new tools rise → "What will you hand off first?"
- **Look:** white/ice desk, flat shapes with soft shadows, navy line work, sky accent on the work card.
- **Sound:** voice, a light upbeat bed, soft taps and paper sounds on every visible action.

## B. Words That Move (the bold one)

**Device:** type carries the argument. **Form:** cut on the beat. Giant Poppins words fill the frame and cut every half beat on sky, navy and ice colour fields; key words turn into the object they name.

- **First 2 s (doc 1):** "PING" "PING" "PING" slam in, stacking until the frame is full of words.
- Each film is 4–5 big kinetic phrases on the voice's beats (doc 2: four giant times, 9:00 · 11:00 · 14:00 · 16:00, each flipping into its result; doc 3: "MANUAL" stutters while "ONE PROMPT" glides; doc 4: IDEA → PROJECT → ARTIFACT → SHIP as word-objects; doc 5: "YOU" + "CLAUDE" lock together).
- **Look:** full-bleed colour fields, huge Poppins Bold, one icon per phrase.
- **Sound:** a punchy beat that the cuts land on, voice on top.
- **Risk:** the most energetic, but the busiest to watch over a Teams share.

## C. Chain Reaction

**Device:** the object is the explanation. **Form:** object-to-object chain: every shot ends on the shape that opens the next, so a bell becomes a search bar, which becomes a chat bubble, which becomes a document.

- **First 2 s (doc 1):** a ringing notification bell swings, then flattens into a search bar.
- Each film is one unbroken chain of 5–7 morphs that follows the voice (doc 4: bulb → folder → window → code brackets → rocket; doc 5: two circles, you and Claude, orbit and merge into one check mark).
- **Look:** big, simple, bright flat shapes on an ice background, with thick navy outlines.
- **Sound:** voice, a light bed, a "whoosh" on each morph.

Assumptions: about 40 s per film, 16:9, the existing voice, free music and sounds only (no paid HeyGen voice, avatar or catalog music). The videos replace the animated HTML scenes on slides 1.3, 3.4, 4.4, 5.4 and 7.4; the HTML versions stay as a fallback. Each finished MP4 is about 5–10 MB.
